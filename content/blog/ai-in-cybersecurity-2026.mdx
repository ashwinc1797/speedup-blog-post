## What is AI in Cybersecurity — Clear Explanation

AI in cybersecurity involves using artificial intelligence technologies, such as machine learning and natural language processing, to detect and respond to security threats. AI-powered security systems can analyze vast amounts of data to identify patterns and anomalies that may indicate a potential threat, similar to a human security analyst reviewing logs and system data. However, AI-powered systems can process much larger amounts of data, much faster, and with greater accuracy. For example, a machine learning-based intrusion detection system can analyze network traffic patterns to identify potential security threats.

To illustrate this concept, consider a security guard monitoring a large building, reviewing security footage, checking for suspicious activity, and responding to potential threats. An AI-powered security system can monitor a network or system, review logs and system data, and respond to potential threats, but with much greater speed and accuracy. This makes it an essential tool in the fight against cyber threats.
