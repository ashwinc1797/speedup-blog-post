## What is Edge AI

Edge AI involves processing and analyzing data at the network's edge, closer to the data source. This approach contrasts with traditional cloud-centric methods, where data is transmitted to the cloud for processing and analysis. An analogy for Edge AI is a car's navigation system, which can process data from sensors like GPS, cameras, and lidar in real-time, allowing for more efficient navigation. Edge AI offers reduced latency, improved security, and enhanced real-time processing capabilities.

In AI development, Edge AI enables more efficient, scalable, and secure applications. For instance, in smart homes, Edge AI can process data from sensors and devices, allowing for real-time automation and control. Companies like Zensar and KPIT in Pune are exploring Edge AI applications in areas like smart homes, industrial automation, and healthcare. The concept of Edge AI has gained traction with the advent of IoT devices and 5G networks, driving the need for Edge AI solutions.

Edge AI has numerous applications across industries, including healthcare, finance, and manufacturing. In healthcare, Edge AI can analyze medical images and diagnose diseases. In finance, Edge AI can detect fraudulent transactions and predict market trends. In manufacturing, Edge AI can predict equipment failures and optimize production processes. Companies like Persistent and Cybage in Pune are exploring Edge AI applications in these areas.
