## What is Explainable AI — Clear Explanation

Explainable AI, also known as XAI, is a subfield of artificial intelligence that focuses on making AI decision-making processes more transparent and interpretable. In simple terms, Explainable AI is like having a black box that can be opened and examined. For instance, imagine using a GPS navigation system that suggests a particular route. With traditional AI, you would follow the route without knowing why it was suggested. With Explainable AI, you would see the exact reasons why the GPS chose that route, such as traffic patterns, road closures, and time of day. This level of transparency is crucial in applications like healthcare, finance, and transportation, where AI decisions can significantly impact people's lives.

In our training sessions, we use real-world examples to explain Explainable AI. For example, we might use a simple machine learning model that predicts house prices based on features like location, size, and number of bedrooms. With Explainable AI, we can see exactly how the model weighs these features to arrive at a prediction. This transparency helps developers identify biases in the model and improve its performance.
